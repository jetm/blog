## About Me

I'm Javier Tia, a software engineer with a focus on systems programming, Linux, and developer tooling. I enjoy working close to the metal — from kernel modules and embedded systems to build infrastructure and automation.

My interests span low-level programming, open-source development, and finding practical solutions to everyday engineering challenges. This blog is where I document what I learn along the way.

## About This Site

This blog covers topics in systems programming, Linux internals, developer workflows, and the tools that make engineering more productive. Posts tend to be hands-on and rooted in real-world problems I've encountered.
